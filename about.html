About N.D. Flow

Built in Nigeria. Built to grow.

N.D. Flow Plumbing Co. started with a simple standard: show up, do the work right, and stand behind it. That's still what runs the business today, on every job — and it's the standard we're building on as the company grows.

Where we work

N.D. Flow is based in Nigeria, with plumbing teams that go wherever the job is — from residential work in Lagos to commercial and construction plumbing in cities including Port Harcourt and Enugu, and across Imo and Anambra States. Every project on this site is real work we've completed, not a showcase piece.

How we work

Plumbing problems don't wait for convenient timing, so neither do we. Our approach is straightforward: clear pricing before work starts, licensed and experienced hands on every job, and a finish that holds up — whether it's a same-day leak repair or a full installation from the ground up.

Where we're headed

Nigeria is where N.D. Flow operates today, and it's where the standard we hold ourselves to was built. From here, the plan is deliberate growth — first across other markets in Africa, and further from there — bringing the same reliability to every new place we work.
